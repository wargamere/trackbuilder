# Career menu artwork

The owner menu uses original fictional racing-driver artwork generated with the built-in image generation tool. The neutral suits work with the team's live primary and accent colours, which control the surrounding lighting, team identity and selected-driver styling. The image is decorative; actual hired-driver names, selected seat, ratings, finances and decisions come from the career save.

Project asset: [team-drivers.webp](../src/assets/career/team-drivers.webp), 1122 × 1402, transparent WebP. The original generated PNG is retained outside the game bundle in `outputs/team-drivers-original.png`. WebP conversion preserves transparency and reduces the game asset to approximately 226 KiB. Vite bundles it locally for both Web and the single-file CrazyGames release.

Final generation prompt:

> Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: transparent hero portrait artwork for a premium motorsport team-management game's career menu, original fictional people. Primary request: two professional racing drivers standing together, upper thighs to top of helmets, front three-quarter view, one in foreground at right with arms crossed and one slightly behind on left with hands at sides. Both wear exquisite charcoal black modern racing suits with subtle silver seam lines and blank small light grey sponsor patches, black gloves, closed full-face racing helmets with dark reflective visors. No identifiable real people. No logos, no brand insignias, no numbers, no letters, no text. Style: polished photorealistic AAA racing-game character render, realistic fabric, realistic proportions, excellent studio character photography, cinematic high contrast. Composition: portrait aspect ratio 4:5, both complete helmets fully inside image with generous clear space at top; foreground driver on right slightly taller in frame; bodies go to bottom edge at mid-thigh, neither helmet nor arms cropped; two silhouettes form a strong sculptural diagonal, leave no props. Lighting: soft neutral white key from upper left, subtle cool silver rim lighting, dark shadows, no coloured lighting. Their neutral charcoal suits should work over any user-selected team colour. Background must be truly transparent alpha, clean isolated cutout, no floor, no gradient, no scene, no cast shadow rectangle.
