# Team owner career

Create a team, choose its name and colours, and sign two drivers, or take over an existing team. Pick either driver for each race. Driver identities, points, qualifying positions and fitted parts remain distinct when you switch control.

The fullscreen menu shares the editor and main menu's paper background, blue ink, rounded controls and yellow primary action. Six primary sections keep the navigation short:

- **Overview:** the next race, the current team decision, driver selection and engineering status. Weekend setup and practice programmes are expandable. Advance to the weekend to move project time forward before qualifying.
- **Engineering:** Research, Development, Assign upgrades, Progress history, Facilities and Vehicle. See [the engineering rules](engineering.md).
- **Drivers:** driver lineup/training, recruitment and specialist staff. One specialist per role; elite staff require 40 reputation. Signing fees are immediate and salaries follow each race.
- **Championship:** standings, calendar, rivalry and career milestones.
- **Team:** finances, sponsorship, team identity and career settings.
- **Inbox:** decisions with visible effects and a saved team journal.

Research, manufacturing and fitting replace the old three generic paid upgrade packages. Resource points unlock designs; money builds copies; only fitted copies affect a car. The old arcade weekend pace/development modifiers are disabled in the new engineering career. Preparation now earns research resources through valid practice programmes, while workforce and facilities control production speed and quality.

Both drivers earn experience and can book one training programme per weekend. Training is delivered after a completed race. Their development, morale and race records stay saved. Specialist staff and the laboratory support research; the coach and simulator support driver training; the race engineer improves AI reliability; commercial facilities add income. Engineering has its own workforce payroll in addition to specialist and department operating costs.

Race results update board confidence, morale, reputation and supporters. A season rival is chosen near your car's performance. The head-to-head compares both teams' points after each race, and rival cars receive seeded updates every third race. Meeting the board target pays a season objective bonus once. Career milestones and history carry forward.

Existing saves retain money, drivers, results, grids and earned car performance. A previously paid generic package still completes under its original rules and is shown in Research until delivery. Engineering initializes without a fee or replaying old rewards. At season end, retained fitted gains become per-seat car foundations, and the seasonal research/manufacturing programme restarts at minor upgrades. Unfinished work is refunded proportionally and unfitted stock is recycled. Resource points, workforce, facilities, staff and driver development persist. See the engineering rules for regulation adaptation and refund details.

Mechanical failure probabilities apply to AI-controlled cars. The selected player's car is never forced to retire by a scripted failure. Operating expenses settle once per completed race. Insufficient cash releases specialist contracts after charging the available balance; no hidden debt is created.

## Season calendars

Before the first on-track session of each season, open **Configure season calendar** on Overview or **Championship → Calendar**. Choose any 1–30 unique real circuits, add or remove races, reorder them, or start from an 8/16/24/30-round preset. Save applies the whole draft at once; Discard leaves the saved schedule intact. Starting practice, qualifying or the race locks the season, including practice with no completed lap. Completed results cannot be moved to different circuits. A custom schedule carries into the next preseason and can be edited again; the season-review action opens that editor.

## Car performance and expert difficulty

Car performance uses the earlier, milder balance for every entrant, including the selected driver and AI teammate. A 52% base car has 89.44% of the reference grip, 85.6% of its engine pull and 95.2% of its engine speed ceiling, before fitted parts and driver development. Qualifying, race cars, lane planning and the player's braking advice use those capabilities. No finishing position is assigned from the car rating.

The existing 1–100 difficulty pace curve remains unchanged. Levels 101–110 are an additional expert challenge: at 110 the AI receives 8% more grip, 10% more engine pull and a 4% higher engine ceiling, and uses the full brakes. The bonus scales in over those ten levels and applies consistently to qualifying and racing; it is not a catch-up adjustment. Difficulty up to 110 persists in career and quick-race settings. Norrin's base roster rating is 90.

## Longer careers

Vehicle comparison uses **performance index (PI)** rather than a percentage of the leader. The initial top car is 700 PI; no team is rescaled to 100% at a season boundary. Rivals bring small seeded updates and retain absolute progress. Your fitted and integrated improvements contribute to your two-car average, while physics continues to use each seat's own hardware. Gains above the reference car have diminishing physical returns, so the programme can repeat over many seasons without unlimited straight-line speeds.

Weather is seeded by season and circuit. Desert venues, including Las Vegas, Sakhir, Jeddah, Lusail and Yas Marina, have a 1.5% wet-weekend candidate chance; wetter venues have 24%, others 16%. A season has at most one wet candidate per four scheduled races (at least one slot for very short custom calendars), and never three consecutive wet weekends. Only a quarter of selected wet weekends are rain throughout; the others have changing conditions. Reopening or reloading cannot reroll the forecast.

## Driver negotiations

The Drivers section allows talks with multiple candidates throughout the year, including after qualifying. Offers include an immediate signing fee, a salary per race and a one-to-three-season term. Drivers counter offers below their requested terms; quotes reflect your car, reputation, renewals and any replacement buyout. Talks last three further completed rounds and can be reopened. An acceptance reserves that driver and seat for next season, pays the fee once and keeps two salaries in reserve. Cancelling a future agreement forfeits its fee.

No accepted move changes the current lineup, qualifying grid, results or points. Agreements take effect only after the final race and season archive. Car foundations stay with the physical seats; driver development stays with the person. Existing longer contracts keep their term and salary. An unfilled expiring seat automatically retains its current driver on a one-year continuation at 15% above negotiated renewal salary; swapping teammates keeps the field unique. First-year founding contracts are prepaid. Later active salaries appear in Finance and settle once per completed round; inactive future deals do not charge wages.

## Season awards and archive

The full-screen recap celebrates constructors' results and both drivers' championships, combined team points/wins/podiums, best weekend, car-index gains, research/fitting progress and the following season's agreements. Prize money is $1m plus $350k per constructor beaten, with a further $1m for an owned driver's title. This is separate from the existing board-objective bonus. The award is guarded in the save and cannot be replayed by reopening the screen, reloading or calling the season transition again.

Up to 200 completed-season reports retain the team's name, both drivers' names/results, performance index and prize, even after later renames or transfers. Older saves begin tracking index gains when this update first sees them; a late baseline is labelled instead of inventing earlier development data. The recap's next-season action opens the calendar editor.

The owner/two-driver structure and negotiation reference is [EA's My Team 2.0 career overview](https://www.ea.com/games/f1/f1-25/news/f1-25-career-deep-dive); timing, seasonal programmes, prices and balance here implement Track Builder's own rules.
