# Team owner career

Create a team, choose its name and colours, and sign two drivers, or take over an existing team. Pick either driver for each race. Driver identities, points, qualifying positions and fitted parts remain distinct when you switch control.

The fullscreen menu shares the editor and main menu's paper background, blue ink, rounded controls and yellow primary action. Six primary sections keep the navigation short:

- **Overview:** the next race, the current team decision, driver selection and engineering status. Weekend setup and practice programmes are expandable. Advance to the weekend to move project time forward before qualifying.
- **Engineering:** Research, Development, Assign upgrades, Progress history, Facilities and Vehicle. See [the engineering rules](engineering.md).
- **Drivers:** driver lineup/training, recruitment and specialist staff. One specialist per role; elite staff require 40 reputation. Signing fees are immediate and salaries follow each race.
- **Championship:** standings, calendar, rivalry and career milestones.
- **Team:** finances, sponsorship, team identity and career settings.
- **Inbox:** decisions with visible effects and a saved team journal.

Research, manufacturing and fitting replace the old three generic paid upgrade packages. Resource points unlock designs; money builds copies; only fitted copies affect a car. The old arcade weekend pace/development modifiers are disabled in the new engineering career. Preparation now earns research resources through valid practice programmes, while workforce and facilities control production speed and quality.

Both drivers earn experience and can book one training programme per weekend. Training is delivered after a completed race. Their development, morale and race records stay saved. Specialist staff and the laboratory support research; the coach and simulator support driver training; the race engineer improves AI reliability; commercial facilities add income. Engineering has its own workforce payroll in addition to specialist and department operating costs.

Race results update board confidence, morale, reputation and supporters. A season rival is chosen near your car's performance. The head-to-head compares both teams' points after each race, and rival cars receive seeded updates every third race. Meeting the board target pays a season objective bonus once. Career milestones and history carry forward.

Existing saves retain money, drivers, results, grids and earned car performance. A previously paid generic package still completes under its original rules and is shown in Research until delivery. Engineering initializes without a fee or replaying old rewards. Research designs, stock, physical-car fitments, projects, resource points, workforce and facilities persist; unadapted parts in a regulation-affected department are removed at the season boundary. The seasonal spending allowance resets.

Mechanical failure probabilities apply to AI-controlled cars. The selected player's car is never forced to retire by a scripted failure. Operating expenses settle once per completed race. Insufficient cash releases specialist contracts after charging the available balance; no hidden debt is created.

## Season calendars

Before the first on-track session of each season, open **Configure season calendar** on Overview or **Championship → Calendar**. Choose any 1–30 unique real circuits, add or remove races, reorder them, or start from an 8/16/24/30-round preset. Save applies the whole draft at once; Discard leaves the saved schedule intact. Starting practice, qualifying or the race locks the season, including practice with no completed lap. Completed results cannot be moved to different circuits. A custom schedule carries into the next preseason and can be edited again; the season-review action opens that editor.

## Car performance and expert difficulty

The physical car gap is larger for every entrant, including the selected driver and AI teammate. A 52% base car has 76% of the reference grip, 71.2% of its engine pull and 88% of its engine speed ceiling, before fitted parts and driver development. Qualifying, race cars, lane planning and the player's braking advice use those capabilities. No finishing position is assigned from the car rating.

The existing 1–100 difficulty pace curve remains unchanged. Levels 101–110 are an additional expert challenge: at 110 the AI receives 8% more grip, 10% more engine pull and a 4% higher engine ceiling, and uses the full brakes. The bonus scales in over those ten levels and applies consistently to qualifying and racing; it is not a catch-up adjustment. Difficulty up to 110 persists in career and quick-race settings. Norrin's base roster rating is 90.
